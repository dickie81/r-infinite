**K1. `ψ(x) − x = Ω±(√x)` with no hypothesis (E. Schmidt, 1903 [recalled]).**
- Socket: `CrossCriteria.PsiOmegaSqrt` (`src/CrossCriteria.lean:125`): there is `c > 0` with `ψ(x) − x > c√x` and `ψ(x) − x < −c√x` for arbitrarily large `x`.
- Landed: round 509's J3, `HalfPlaneJoinsPNT.psiOmegaSqrt_of_not_RH : ¬RiemannHypothesis → CrossCriteria.PsiOmegaSqrt` (`external/pnt/HalfPlaneJoinsPNT.lean:48`).
- Missing Prop: `RiemannHypothesis → CrossCriteria.PsiOmegaSqrt`. With J3 it gives `PsiOmegaSqrt` with no hypothesis.
- Route (PAPER, [recalled: Landau's comparison]): under RH, suppose `ψ(x) − x ≤ c√x` for `x ≥ X`. Up to an entire term, the Mellin transform of the nonnegative `c√x − (ψ(x) − x)` is `c/(s − ½) + ζ′(s)/(sζ(s)) + 1/(s − 1)`, analytic on `Re s > ½`. Near a zero `ρ₀ = ½ + iγ₀` of order `m ≥ 1` it is `m/(ρ₀(s − ρ₀))` plus a bounded term. By positivity its modulus at `σ + iγ₀` is at most its value at `σ`, which is `c/(σ − ½)` plus a bounded term, since `ζ(½) ≠ 0`. As `σ → ½⁺` this gives `c ≥ m/|ρ₀|`, so `c = 1/(2|ρ₀|)` fails; the lower side is the same argument.
- The stack's `PsiOmega.psi_omega` (`src/PsiOmega.lean:655`) is the strict case `θ < Re ρ`, through the `PoleAt` hypothesis of `zeta_ne_zero_of_mellin`. The case `θ = Re ρ = ½` needs the residue comparison; the register recorded this gap before round 515: "needs a zero *on* `Re = ½` with the Landau argument at the real singularity, which `PoleAt`-at-a-zero does not capture".
